# Mac Admins India City Connect - Sponsor Terms & Conditions

**Version:** 1.1  
**Published by:** Mac Admins India Team

## 1. About Mac Admins India City Connect

**Mac Admins India City Connect** is a series of community-led, in-person meetups held across different cities in India. These events bring Apple IT professionals together locally to connect, share technical knowledge, learn from one another, and strengthen the community.

Individual events are named using the format **Mac Admins India City Connect - <CityName>**.

These Terms & Conditions apply to sponsors, venue hosts, partners, and other organisations supporting a Mac Admins India City Connect.

The City Connect remains a **Mac Admins India community event** even when supported or sponsored by a vendor.

## 2. Sponsor Session

As part of the sponsor arrangement, the sponsoring organisation may have an agreed speaking session at the City Connect.

Sponsor sessions should provide meaningful technical and educational value to the Apple IT community.

Topics may include:

- Apple platform technologies
- Device management and MDM
- Security and compliance
- Identity and authentication
- Automation and scripting
- Deployment and implementation
- Troubleshooting and operational workflows
- Customer experiences or implementation learnings
- Product capabilities relevant to Apple IT professionals
- Industry knowledge, challenges, and best practices

As City Connects are primarily technical and knowledge-sharing events, sponsor sessions should be **technical and educational rather than primarily sales or marketing presentations**.

Reasonable product demonstrations and information about the sponsor's products or services are permitted where relevant to the technical topic.

## 3. Community Speaker Sessions

In addition to the sponsor session, speaking opportunities will be made available to members of the Mac Admins India community.

Community speakers are free to present topics based on their experience, knowledge, projects, workflows, or technologies they work with.

Topics may include Apple technologies, device management, MDM, security, identity, automation, scripting, deployment, employee experience, open-source tools, commercial products, or other subjects relevant to Apple IT professionals.

## 4. Vendor-Neutral Community Content

Mac Admins India is committed to maintaining an open and vendor-neutral environment for community knowledge sharing.

Community speakers may discuss, demonstrate, compare, or share genuine professional experience with products, vendors, platforms, tools, and technologies, including technologies that compete with those offered by the event sponsor.

A sponsor must not require community speakers to avoid mentioning, comparing, or presenting about competing products or technologies.

Hosting a Mac Admins India City Connect at a sponsor's office or sponsored venue does not provide the sponsor with editorial control over community presentations.

For example, if Vendor A sponsors a City Connect, an independent community practitioner may present about Vendor B's technology based on their own professional experience.

## 5. Competing Vendor Participation

While Mac Admins India maintains a vendor-neutral approach to community knowledge sharing, it also recognises the commercial relationship with the sponsor supporting a particular City Connect.

Employees or representatives of other vendors, including organisations that compete with the event sponsor, are welcome to register for and attend the City Connect as attendees, subject to the same registration requirements and Code of Conduct as other participants.

However, employees or representatives of another vendor may not use a City Connect sponsored by a competing vendor to:

- Deliver a presentation or technical session.
- Participate as an official speaker or panellist.
- Conduct a product demonstration.
- Promote or present their company's products or services.
- Operate a booth or other vendor activity.
- Conduct organised sales or marketing activities.

This restriction is intended to avoid commercial conflicts of interest and applies when the individual is participating in their professional or vendor representative capacity.

This restriction does not prevent an individual from participating as a normal attendee, including reasonable networking, asking questions, or joining community discussions, provided they follow the Code of Conduct and do not use the event for competing vendor promotion.

For example, if Vendor A sponsors a City Connect, an employee of Vendor B may attend the event, but would not be invited to present, demonstrate Vendor B's products, participate as a speaker or panellist, or conduct Vendor B promotional activities at that event.

## 6. Speaker Selection and Agenda

The **Mac Admins India Team** will coordinate the overall City Connect agenda and community speaker selection.

Sponsor representatives may suggest topics or speakers, but the final agenda, community speaker selection, session order, and event format will be determined by the Mac Admins India Team.

The Mac Admins India Team may modify the agenda where necessary to maintain a balanced and useful community event.

The Mac Admins India Team retains the discretion to determine whether a proposed speaker is participating as an independent community practitioner or as a representative of a vendor or commercial organisation.

## 7. Inviting Customers, Partners and Guests

Sponsors are welcome and encouraged to invite their customers, partners, prospects, employees, and other Apple IT professionals who may benefit from participating in the City Connect.

All attendees must follow the applicable event registration process.

Attendance may be limited based on venue capacity or other operational requirements.

Participation in the City Connect does not require attendees to be customers or partners of the sponsor.

Invited guests attend as community participants unless separately approved for an official event role.

## 8. No Sales Restrictions on Attendees

Attendees should be able to participate freely in community discussions regardless of which products, vendors, or technologies their organizations currently use.

Sponsors must not restrict attendance or participation based on an attendee's employer, vendor relationship, technology choices, or use of competing products.

The competing-vendor participation restriction in Section 5 applies specifically to individuals participating in their professional/vendor representative capacity and does not prevent ordinary attendance or community participation.

## 9. Code of Conduct

All attendees, speakers, sponsors, guests, and members of the Mac Admins India Team must follow the [Mac Admins India Community Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Respectful and professional behaviour is expected throughout the City Connect.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, inappropriate behaviour, or deliberate disruption of sessions or community discussions will not be tolerated.

The Mac Admins India Team reserves the right to take appropriate action where the Code of Conduct is not followed.

## 10. Venue and Logistics

Where the sponsor is providing the venue, the sponsor should ensure that the agreed meeting space and required facilities are available for the duration of the City Connect.

Venue-specific requirements, including security procedures, visitor registration, identification requirements, accessibility, parking, internet access, or building access, should be communicated to the Mac Admins India Team in advance.

Any venue restrictions that may materially affect the City Connect should be discussed and agreed with the Mac Admins India Team before the event is announced.

The sponsor or venue host remains responsible for venue-specific rules, facilities, and operational requirements under its control.

## 11. Event Branding

The event should be communicated as a **Mac Admins India City Connect**, with appropriate acknowledgement of the sponsor.

Mac Admins India branding, logos, event artwork, or other community assets must not be modified or used outside the agreed City Connect promotion without approval from the Mac Admins India Team.

Sponsorship of a City Connect does not imply endorsement by Mac Admins India of a particular company, product, or service.

The sponsor must not describe the event in a way that suggests the sponsor owns or controls the Mac Admins India community or its content.

## 12. Event Promotion

Mac Admins India may promote the City Connect through its official community channels.

Sponsors are also welcome to promote the City Connect through their own channels and invite relevant participants.

Public communications should accurately describe the event as a **Mac Admins India City Connect** and acknowledge the sponsor appropriately.

The sponsor is responsible for ensuring that promotional information it provides is accurate and current.

## 13. Photography and Recording

Photos or videos may be taken during the City Connect for community, sponsor, and event promotional purposes.

Where photography or recording is planned, attendees should be appropriately informed.

Any recording of complete speaker sessions should be coordinated with the Mac Admins India Team and the respective speaker.

Sponsors and venue hosts should not commercially reuse community speaker content, recordings, or presentations without appropriate permission.

Speakers are responsible for the content and accuracy of their presentations.

Opinions expressed by speakers or attendees are their own and do not necessarily represent the views of Mac Admins India, the Mac Admins India Team, the sponsor, the venue, or their respective employers.

Mac Admins India, the Mac Admins India Team, sponsors, and venue hosts do not independently validate every technical statement, recommendation, configuration, script, demonstration, or opinion presented during a session.

Attendees should independently evaluate technical guidance before applying it to their own environments.

Sponsors or venue hosts must not record, reproduce, distribute, modify, or commercially use a community speaker's presentation without the speaker's permission.

Use of speaker material outside the specific City Connect should be agreed with the relevant speaker.

## 16. Attendee Information, Registration Data and Marketing

Any attendee information shared with or made accessible to a sponsor, partner, or venue host for the purpose of registration, check-in, venue access, security, catering, or event administration must be used only for the specific purpose for which it was provided.

Sponsors, partners, and venue hosts must **not use attendee registration information for sales, marketing, lead generation, promotional communications, mailing lists, or any unrelated purpose** unless the individual attendee has separately and explicitly provided consent.

Attendee information must not be copied, retained, exported, shared with third parties, added to CRM or marketing systems, or otherwise reused beyond the agreed purpose.

Where attendee information is required temporarily for event operations or venue access, it should be securely handled and deleted when it is no longer reasonably required for that purpose, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.

Sponsors may provide their own **optional and clearly identified** mechanisms during the event for attendees who voluntarily wish to share their contact information, request a demonstration, subscribe to communications, enter a promotion, or engage with the sponsor.

Participation in such sponsor activities must be voluntary and separate from Mac Admins India City Connect registration.

If a City Connect needs to be postponed, relocated, or cancelled, the Mac Admins India Team and sponsor will make reasonable efforts to communicate the change to registered attendees.

Sponsors should make reasonable efforts to support the event once it has been publicly announced and should provide timely information about any material changes or cancellation.

Repeated cancellations, significant last-minute changes, or failure to deliver agreed sponsor commitments may affect eligibility for future Mac Admins India City Connect sponsorship opportunities.
