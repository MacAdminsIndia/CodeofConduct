# Mac Admins India City Connect – Sponsors Terms & Conditions

**Mac Admins India City Connect** is a series of community-led, in-person meetups that brings Apple IT professionals together locally to connect, share technical knowledge, learn from one another, and strengthen the Apple IT community.

Mac Admins India City Connect events will take place across different cities in India, creating opportunities for community members to meet and collaborate in their local regions.

Individual events may be named using the format **Mac Admins India City Connect - <CityName>**.

These Terms & Conditions apply to Mac Admins India City Connect events conducted with the support of sponsors, partners, or venue hosts.

The purpose of these meetups is to bring Apple IT professionals together to **Engage. Empower. Elevate.** through technical knowledge sharing, networking, and community collaboration.

## 1. Community-Led Event

Mac Admins India City Connect events are community-led events managed by the **Mac Admins India Team**.

A sponsor may support the City Connect by providing the venue, refreshments, logistics, or other agreed support. Sponsorship or hosting does not change the community-led nature of the event.

The meetup should not be presented as a sponsor-owned, sponsor-exclusive, or vendor-specific event.

## 2. Sponsor Participation

The sponsor will receive a speaking slot as part of the City Connect agenda, with the duration and schedule agreed with the Mac Admins India Team.

Sponsors are encouraged to use their session to share:

- Technical knowledge and demonstrations
- Practical use cases and workflows
- Customer experiences or implementation learnings
- Product capabilities relevant to Apple IT professionals
- Industry knowledge, challenges, and best practices

As Mac Admins India City Connects are primarily technical and knowledge-sharing events, we strongly encourage sponsor sessions to be **technical and educational rather than primarily sales or marketing presentations**.

## 3. Community Speaker Sessions

In addition to the sponsor session, speaking opportunities will be made available to members of the Mac Admins India community.

Community speakers are free to present topics based on their experience, knowledge, projects, workflows, or technologies they work with.

Topics may include Apple technologies, device management, MDM, security, identity, automation, scripting, deployment, employee experience, open-source tools, commercial products, or other subjects relevant to Apple IT professionals.

## 4. Vendor-Neutral Community Content

There must be **no restriction on which products, vendors, platforms, or technologies community members may discuss**.

Community speakers may discuss products or technologies that compete with those offered by the sponsor or venue host.

Hosting a Mac Admins India City Connect at a sponsor's office or sponsored venue does not provide the sponsor with editorial control over community presentations.

For example, a community member may present about one vendor's technology while the City Connect is being hosted at the office of another vendor.

This vendor-neutral approach is an important part of maintaining an open and useful technical community.

## 5. Speaker Selection and Agenda

The **Mac Admins India Team** will coordinate the overall City Connect agenda and community speaker selection.

Sponsor representatives may suggest topics or speakers, but the final agenda, community speaker selection, session order, and event format will be determined by the Mac Admins India Team.

The Mac Admins India Team may modify the agenda where necessary to maintain a balanced and useful community event.

## 6. Inviting Customers, Partners and Guests

Sponsors are welcome and encouraged to invite their customers, partners, prospects, employees, and other Apple IT professionals who may benefit from participating in the City Connect.

All attendees must follow the applicable event registration process.

Attendance may be limited based on venue capacity or other operational requirements.

Participation in the City Connect does not require attendees to be customers or partners of the sponsor.

## 7. No Sales Restrictions on Attendees

Attendees should be able to participate freely in community discussions regardless of which products, vendors, or technologies their organizations currently use.

Sponsors must not restrict attendance or participation based on an attendee's employer, vendor relationship, technology choices, or use of competing products.

## 8. Code of Conduct

All attendees, speakers, sponsors, guests, and members of the Mac Admins India Team must follow the [Mac Admins India Code of Conduct](https://github.com/MacAdminsIndia/CodeofConduct/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Respectful and professional behaviour is expected throughout the City Connect.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, inappropriate behaviour, or deliberate disruption of sessions or community discussions will not be tolerated.

The Mac Admins India Team reserves the right to take appropriate action where the Code of Conduct is not followed.

## 9. Venue and Logistics

Where the sponsor is providing the venue, the sponsor should ensure that the agreed meeting space and required facilities are available for the duration of the City Connect.

Venue-specific requirements, including security procedures, visitor registration, identification requirements, accessibility, parking, internet access, or building access, should be communicated to the Mac Admins India Team in advance.

Any venue restrictions that may materially affect the City Connect should be discussed and agreed with the Mac Admins India Team before the event is announced.

## 10. Event Branding

The event should be communicated as a **Mac Admins India City Connect**, with appropriate acknowledgement of the sponsor.

Mac Admins India branding, logos, event artwork, or other community assets must not be modified or used outside the agreed City Connect promotion without approval from the Mac Admins India Team.

Sponsorship of a City Connect does not imply endorsement by Mac Admins India of a particular company, product, or service.

## 11. Event Promotion

Mac Admins India may promote the City Connect through its official community channels.

Sponsors are also welcome to promote the City Connect through their own channels and invite relevant participants.

Public communications should accurately describe the event as a **Mac Admins India City Connect** and acknowledge the sponsor appropriately.

## 12. Photography and Recording

Photos or videos may be taken during the City Connect for community, sponsor, and event promotional purposes.

Where photography or recording is planned, attendees should be appropriately informed.

Any recording of complete speaker sessions should be coordinated with the Mac Admins India Team and the respective speaker.

## 13. Speaker Content and Opinions

Speakers are responsible for the content and accuracy of their presentations.

Opinions expressed by speakers or attendees are their own and do not necessarily represent the views of Mac Admins India, the Mac Admins India Team, the sponsor, the venue, or their respective employers.

## 14. Intellectual Property

Speakers retain ownership of their original presentation content unless otherwise agreed.

Mac Admins India may request permission from speakers to share presentation slides, session recordings, photographs, or other materials with the wider community.

Sponsors or venue hosts must not record, reproduce, distribute, or commercially use a community speaker's presentation without the speaker's permission.

## 15. Attendee Information, Registration Data and Marketing

Any attendee information shared with or made accessible to a sponsor, partner, or venue host for the purpose of registration, check-in, venue access, security, catering, or event administration must be used only for the specific purpose for which it was provided.

Sponsors, partners, and venue hosts must **not use attendee registration information for sales, marketing, lead generation, promotional communications, mailing lists, or any unrelated purpose** unless the individual attendee has separately and explicitly provided consent.

Attendee information must not be copied, retained, exported, shared with third parties, added to CRM or marketing systems, or otherwise reused beyond the agreed purpose.

Where attendee information is required temporarily for event operations or venue access, it should be securely handled and deleted when it is no longer reasonably required for that purpose.

Sponsors may provide their own **optional and clearly identified** mechanisms during the event for attendees who voluntarily wish to share their contact information, request a demonstration, subscribe to communications, enter a promotion, or engage with the sponsor.

Participation in such sponsor activities must be voluntary and separate from Mac Admins India City Connect registration.

## 16. Event Changes or Cancellation

The **Mac Admins India Team** may update the venue, agenda, timing, speaker lineup, registration capacity, or event format where required due to operational, safety, community, or logistical reasons.

If a City Connect needs to be postponed, relocated, or cancelled, the Mac Admins India Team and sponsor will make reasonable efforts to communicate the change to registered attendees.

## 17. Liability

Participation in the City Connect is voluntary and at the attendee's own risk to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Mac Admins India, the Mac Admins India Team, sponsors, and venue hosts are not responsible for personal loss, damage, travel disruption, or circumstances outside their reasonable control.

## 18. Community Independence

Sponsorship is an important contribution that helps Mac Admins India conduct accessible City Connects across India.

At the same time, sponsorship does not provide ownership or editorial control over the Mac Admins India community, its members, discussions, community speaker content, or technology choices.

Mac Admins India is committed to maintaining an **open, inclusive, vendor-neutral environment** where Apple IT professionals can freely exchange knowledge and experiences.

## 19. Final Decision

The **Mac Admins India Team** has final responsibility for the City Connect format, agenda, community speaker selection, event communications, Code of Conduct matters, and other community-related decisions.

The Mac Admins India Team may update these Terms & Conditions when required to support the community and its events.

## 20. Acceptance

By sponsoring, hosting, speaking at, registering for, or attending a Mac Admins India City Connect, the relevant participant agrees to follow these Terms & Conditions and the Mac Admins India Code of Conduct.
