## Community Code of Conduct

All members participating in Mac Admins India community spaces and activities are expected to follow the [Mac Admins India Community Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

The Code of Conduct applies across official community spaces and activities, including WhatsApp, virtual events, Mac Admins India City Connect, Sponsor Technical Workshops promoted by Mac Admins India, Mac Admins India Connect, GitHub community spaces, networking activities, and other official community initiatives.

WhatsApp-specific operational expectations are covered separately in the [WhatsApp Community Guidelines](WHATSAPP_COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md).

## Mac Admins India City Connect

**Mac Admins India City Connect** is our series of community-led, in-person meetups happening across different cities in India, bringing Apple IT professionals together locally to connect, share technical knowledge, learn, and collaborate.

Individual events are named using the format **Mac Admins India City Connect - `<CityName>`**.

City Connect events may be supported by sponsors and venue partners while remaining community-led and vendor-neutral.

### Terms & Conditions

Please review the applicable Terms & Conditions before participating in a Mac Admins India City Connect event:

-   Sponsors: [City Connect Sponsor Terms & Conditions](CITY_CONNECT_SPONSORS_TERMS.md)
-   Attendees: [City Connect Attendee Terms & Conditions](CITY_CONNECT_ATTENDEE_TERMS.md)

By sponsoring, hosting, registering for, speaking at, or attending a Mac Admins India City Connect event, participants are expected to follow the applicable Terms & Conditions and the Mac Admins India Community Code of Conduct.

## Mac Admins India Sponsor Technical Workshops

**Mac Admins India Sponsor Technical Workshops** are free technical workshops and training sessions independently organised and delivered by eligible **Mac Admins India Connect sponsors** and promoted to the Mac Admins India community.

These workshops provide Apple IT professionals with opportunities to gain practical technical knowledge and hands-on experience from organisations that support Mac Admins India Connect.

The sponsoring vendor is responsible for organising and managing the workshop, including registration, venue, trainers, technical content, labs, attendee communications, certificates, and other workshop arrangements. Mac Admins India's role is primarily to **promote eligible workshops to the community**.

Workshops promoted under this program must be **free for attendees** and should provide meaningful technical and educational content rather than primarily serving as sales or marketing events.

Sponsors are expected, wherever reasonably practical, to provide attendees who complete the workshop with a **Certificate of Participation or Certificate of Completion** at no additional cost.

Registration and participation are voluntary and are the responsibility of each attendee. Promotion by Mac Admins India does not constitute endorsement of the vendor, its products or services, or the workshop's technical content.

### Guidelines & Terms & Conditions

Sponsors/vendors requesting promotional support and attendees registering for or participating in a Sponsor Technical Workshop should review the [Sponsor Technical Workshops - Guidelines & Terms & Conditions](SPONSOR_TECHNICAL_WORKSHOP_TERMS.md).
