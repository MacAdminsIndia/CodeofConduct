## Mac Admins India City Connect

*Mac Admins India City Connect* is our series of community-led,
in-person meetups happening across different cities in India, bringing
Apple IT professionals together locally to connect, share technical
knowledge, learn, and collaborate.

Individual events are named Mac Admins India City Connect - .

City Connect events may be supported by sponsors and venue partners
while remaining community-led and vendor-neutral.

## Mac Admins India Sponsor Technical Workshops

**Mac Admins India Sponsor Technical Workshops** are free technical
workshops and training sessions independently organised and delivered by
eligible **Mac Admins India Connect sponsors** and promoted to the Mac
Admins India community.

These workshops provide Apple IT professionals with opportunities to
gain practical technical knowledge and hands-on experience from
organisations that support Mac Admins India Connect.

The sponsoring vendor is responsible for organising and managing the
workshop, including registrations, venue, trainers, technical content,
labs, attendee communications, certificates, and other workshop
arrangements. Mac Admins India's role is primarily to **promote eligible
workshops to the community**.

Workshops promoted under this program must be **free for attendees** and
should provide meaningful technical and educational content rather than
primarily serving as sales or marketing events.

Sponsors are encouraged to provide attendees who complete the workshop
with a **Certificate of Participation or Certificate of Completion** at
no additional cost.

Registration and participation are voluntary and are the responsibility
of each attendee. Promotion by Mac Admins India does not constitute
endorsement of the vendor, its products or services, or the workshop's
technical content.
