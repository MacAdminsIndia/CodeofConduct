# Mac Admins India WhatsApp Community Guidelines

> **Engage. Empower. Elevate.**

The Mac Admins India WhatsApp Community is a space for Apple IT professionals to connect, learn, share knowledge, discover opportunities, and support fellow community members.

These guidelines supplement the [Mac Admins India Community Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). All members are expected to follow both.

## 1. Use the Appropriate Group

### MAI: Discussions 🛠️

Use this group for Apple IT discussions, troubleshooting, MDM, macOS/iOS management, security, automation, tools, questions, and knowledge sharing.

### Jobs / Career Groups

Use these groups for relevant Apple IT job opportunities and career-related discussions.

### City / Regional Groups

These groups are primarily for local meetups, city-specific updates, networking, and helping community members connect locally.

Technical discussions should remain in **MAI: Discussions 🛠️** where possible so the wider community can benefit.

## 2. City and Regional Groups

Official Mac Admins India meetup announcements may be shared community-wide, while local details such as venues, coordination, photos, travel suggestions, and informal gatherings can take place in the relevant city group.

Members may join any city group and do not have to live in that city. Members travelling to another city are welcome to join its group and connect with local Mac Admins.

City groups are intended to improve local collaboration and are not intended to divide the wider Mac Admins India community.

## 3. No Spam or Unsolicited Promotion

Do not post:

-   Repeated promotional messages.
-   Unrelated advertisements.
-   Referral or affiliate links.
-   Mass-forwarded messages.
-   Unsolicited sales or marketing content.
-   Irrelevant products, services, events, or groups.

Relevant Apple IT resources, tools, events, and community initiatives are welcome when shared genuinely for community benefit.

Vendors should avoid unsolicited product promotion or privately contacting members for sales purposes.

## 4. Jobs Stay in the Jobs Board

Apple IT job opportunities and relevant career posts should be shared in the designated Jobs Board or career group rather than unrelated discussion or city groups.

## 5. Respect Member Privacy

Do not collect, export, share, or reuse members' phone numbers, profile information, messages, screenshots, or other contact information for unrelated purposes without appropriate permission.

Do not share private messages or screenshots of conversations outside their intended context without permission.

Do not add people to groups or distribute group invitation links outside the intended community process without authorisation.

## 6. Private Messages

Being in the same WhatsApp Community does not automatically give permission for unsolicited private sales, marketing, recruitment spam, or other inappropriate direct messages.

Professional one-to-one communication is welcome where it is relevant and respectful of the recipient's boundaries.

## 7. Keep It Professional and Safe for Work

Do not share offensive, sexually explicit, violent, discriminatory, or otherwise inappropriate content.

Avoid political, religious, or other highly divisive discussions unless directly relevant to the professional purpose of the community.

## 8. Help Each Other

Questions from all experience levels are welcome.

When asking for technical help, provide useful context where possible. When you find a solution, consider sharing it back with the community so others can learn from it.

## 9. Follow WhatsApp's Rules

Members must comply with WhatsApp's Terms of Service and applicable laws.

## 10. Moderation

Community admins may intervene when these guidelines or the Mac Admins India Community Code of Conduct are not followed.

Depending on the situation, action may include a reminder, warning, restriction from a group, or removal from the Mac Admins India WhatsApp Community.

Serious harassment, abuse, spam, privacy violations, or behaviour that puts community members at risk may result in immediate removal.

If you experience or observe inappropriate behaviour, contact a Mac Admins India community admin privately.
