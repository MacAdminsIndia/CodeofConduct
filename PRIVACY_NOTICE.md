# Mac Admins India Community Privacy Notice

**Last updated: 11 August 2026**

This notice explains the general privacy principles Mac Admins India follows across its community spaces and activities. Specific events, registration platforms, sponsors, vendors, venues, or service providers may provide additional privacy information that also applies.

## 1. Information We May Use

Depending on the activity, Mac Admins India may receive information voluntarily provided by community members or attendees, such as:

-   Name and professional information.
-   Email address or other contact information.
-   Event registration and check-in information.
-   City or regional preferences.
-   Feedback submitted through community or event forms.
-   Photographs or video captured at community events.
-   Information provided when contacting the Mac Admins India Team.

Mac Admins India aims to collect and use only information reasonably relevant to the community activity involved.

## 2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for legitimate community and event purposes, including:

-   Event registration and administration.
-   Check-in and capacity management.
-   Operational and event communications.
-   Community announcements.
-   Responding to questions or requests.
-   Community planning and feedback.
-   Safety, security, venue access, or catering where required.
-   Communicating relevant Mac Admins India activities.

## 3. Sponsors, Partners and Venue Hosts

Mac Admins India does not treat participation in the community or an event as automatic consent for personal information to be provided to sponsors for their independent sales or marketing activities.

Where attendee information is shared with a sponsor, partner, or venue host for a specific operational purpose such as check-in, security, venue access, or catering, it should be used only for that purpose and handled in accordance with the applicable event terms.

Sponsors may provide optional and clearly identified ways for attendees to voluntarily share information directly with them.

## 4. Sponsor Technical Workshops

Sponsor Technical Workshops are independently organised by eligible Mac Admins India Connect sponsors.

Registration for these workshops is generally handled directly by the sponsoring vendor. Information submitted through the vendor's registration system is controlled and handled by that vendor under its own applicable privacy practices.

Mac Admins India does not control information voluntarily submitted directly to a vendor.

Attendees should review the vendor's privacy information before registering.

## 5. WhatsApp Community

Participation in WhatsApp may make certain profile and contact information visible to other participants according to WhatsApp's features and privacy settings.

Community members must not harvest, export, share, or reuse another member's contact information for unsolicited marketing or unrelated purposes.

Members should review their own WhatsApp privacy settings and the [WhatsApp Community Guidelines](WHATSAPP_COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md).

## 6. Photography and Recording

Photography or video may take place at Mac Admins India events for community, event, and promotional purposes.

Where reasonably practical, attendees who have concerns about appearing in event photography should inform the Mac Admins India Team or follow event-specific instructions.

Complete session recordings or other recordings may be subject to additional speaker, attendee, venue, or event permissions.

## 7. Third-Party Platforms and Services

Mac Admins India activities may use third-party services for registration, communication, forms, event management, or other operational purposes.

Information submitted directly to those services may also be subject to the relevant provider's privacy terms.

## 8. Community Feedback

Mac Admins India may collect feedback to improve events and community activities.

Where feedback is shared with sponsors or partners, unnecessary personal information should not be disclosed unless appropriate for the stated purpose.

## 9. Marketing and Unsolicited Contact

Community membership, event registration, or access to attendee information does not by itself provide sponsors, vendors, partners, or other participants with unrestricted rights to use personal information for sales, lead generation, CRM enrichment, mailing lists, or unrelated marketing.

Where separate marketing consent is required, it should be clearly distinguishable from community or event participation.

## 10. Data Handling

Mac Admins India aims to handle community and event information responsibly and retain it only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable operational, legal, or record-keeping requirements.

## 11. Questions or Concerns

Questions or concerns about how information is handled in a Mac Admins India activity should be raised with the Mac Admins India Team through an official Mac Admins India communication channel.

For information submitted directly to a sponsor, vendor, venue, registration provider, or other third party, attendees may also need to contact that organisation directly.

## 12. Event-Specific Terms

This notice should be read together with any applicable event-specific Terms & Conditions, including:

-   [Mac Admins India City Connect - Sponsor Terms & Conditions](CITY_CONNECT_SPONSORS_TERMS.md)
-   [Mac Admins India City Connect - Attendee Terms & Conditions](CITY_CONNECT_ATTENDEE_TERMS.md)
-   [Mac Admins India Sponsor Technical Workshops - Guidelines & Terms & Conditions](SPONSOR_TECHNICAL_WORKSHOP_TERMS.md)

Where an event-specific document contains more specific requirements for that activity, those requirements apply to that activity.
